# Split files

**Exact historical partition files unavailable.**

The manuscript's 60/20/20 TBX11K assignment (seed and per-image assignment) has not been supplied and cannot be
reconstructed from the published counts. Files generated by `scripts/generate_splits.py` are labelled
`provenance: newly_generated` in their `.meta.json` sidecar and **must not be described as the original split**.

If the authors supply their file, load it with `mars_tb.data.split_manager.load_historical_split(csv, attestation)`
(a written attestation of at least 20 characters is mandatory) and commit it with its SHA-256.
